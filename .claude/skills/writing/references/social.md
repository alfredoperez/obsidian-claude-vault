# Surface: Social

LinkedIn posts, X posts and threads, captions, carousel copy.

Load `core-voice.md` first. Operational format rules (post formats, the format menu, character
counts) live in the `create-social-media-post` skill's `references/linkedin-best-practices.md`. This file is
voice only.

## The One Rule

It has to sound like the author sharing something useful, not an ad. If a line reads like marketing
copy, cut it.

## Hard Rules (this surface)

- **Zero em dashes.** Including captions and carousel slides.
- **Minimal hashtags.** LinkedIn 0-2, and only when they're genuinely a topic. Heavy hashtag stacks
  and pod-style tactics are dead weight. The 2026 algorithm rewards hook, value, and saves.
- **The first 50 words carry the whole load.** LinkedIn truncates, so topic and audience must be
  visible before the fold.
- **No engagement bait.** No "comment 'GUIDE' below", no fake polls, no manufactured controversy.

## Voice on this surface

- Open on the concrete thing: the reversal, the number, the mistake you made.
- One idea per post. If it needs a second idea, it's a second post.
- Concede the downside once, with a number. It buys credibility.
- Close with the link or the invitation, never with a sentimental sign-off.

## Checklist

- [ ] Sounds like the author, not an ad
- [ ] Hook works standing alone, before the "see more" fold
- [ ] Grep for `—`: zero hits
- [ ] Hashtags: 0-2 on LinkedIn, and each one earns its place
- [ ] One idea, one post
- [ ] Ends with a link or an invitation
