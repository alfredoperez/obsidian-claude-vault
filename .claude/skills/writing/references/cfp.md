# Surface: CFP

Conference talk proposals: title, abstract, description, takeaways, speaker bio. Bilingual, English
and Spanish.

Load `core-voice.md` first.

## Hard Rules (this surface)

- **Zero em dashes**, in both languages.
- **Bilingual pairs.** Every CFP ships an English and a Spanish version. The Spanish is a real
  rewrite, not a literal translation: it should read as though it was written in Spanish first.
- **The reviewer is the audience, not the attendee.** A reviewer skims dozens of these. The first
  sentence has to make the talk's value obvious.

## Voice on this surface

- Same problem-first structure as an article: the pain the audience feels, then what the talk gives
  them.
- Concrete over aspirational. "You'll leave knowing how to X" beats "we will explore the landscape
  of X."
- Name the takeaways as things the attendee can do on Monday.
- No hype adjectives ("revolutionary", "game-changing"). The idea has to carry itself.
- The bio is third person, short, and leads with what makes the speaker credible on this specific
  topic, not a career résumé.

## Checklist

- [ ] Problem stated before the solution, in the first two sentences
- [ ] Takeaways are concrete actions, not topics
- [ ] Spanish version reads natively, not as a translation
- [ ] Grep for `—` in both versions: zero hits
- [ ] No hype adjectives
- [ ] Bio leads with topic-specific credibility
